Samora for LinkedIn: privacy

Last updated 3 October 2026.

What the plugin is for

Samora for LinkedIn runs the LinkedIn steps of SAMpaigns that you or your team set up in SamoraOS: visiting a profile, sending an invitation with a note, and sending a message once someone accepts. It only works for people who are signed in to a SamoraOS workspace.

What it reads

  • LinkedIn profiles you open or that your SAMpaign asks it to open: the person's name, headline, company, profile address and connection degree, so it can match them to your SamoraOS records.
  • Your connections list and unread conversations, only when you press "Check accepts and replies" or while Autopilot is on: profile addresses and the names on unread conversations, so it can tell SamoraOS who accepted and who replied. Message text is not read.
  • Your Sent invitations page, at the same moments: only two numbers, how many invitations went out in the last 7 days and how many are still pending, so your weekly limit counts invitations you sent outside Samora too. Names on that page are not read or sent.
  • Your own LinkedIn name, shown in the plugin so you can see which account it runs on.
  • On SamoraOS pages only: a one-time pairing code that the signed-in app hands to the plugin.

It does not read other websites, your browsing history, passwords, or anything on LinkedIn outside the pages above.

What you tell it

  • Your LinkedIn plan (Free, Premium, Sales Navigator or Recruiter Lite), which sets your daily and weekly limits and the length of invitation notes.
  • A one tap label on a LinkedIn reply (Interested, Not now or Not interested), which counts in your SAMpaign results.

Your weekly scorecard in the plugin is built from the counts above and your SamoraOS activity. It shows numbers only.

What it stores and where

A device token that connects this Chrome to your SamoraOS account, your settings, and a short list of recent actions are kept in Chrome's storage on your computer. The people, notes, messages and results are kept in your organisation's SamoraOS workspace, the same place as your SAMpaigns. They are not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for anything other than running and reporting on your SAMpaigns.

The use of information received through this extension adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is sent only over HTTPS, only to your SamoraOS workspace, and is never used to train AI models.

What it sends

In Co-pilot, invitations and messages go out only when you press Enter or LinkedIn's Send button. In Autopilot, which you switch on yourself after confirming the risk, the plugin presses Send for you at a human pace, within your working hours and daily limits. LinkedIn does not allow automated sending, so using Autopilot is your decision about your own account.

If Samora switches LinkedIn Automation off

LinkedIn Automation is an add-on. If it is off for your organisation, the plugin shows a locked screen and reads nothing on LinkedIn until it is switched back on.

Removing your data

Disconnect the plugin from its Settings, or remove it from Chrome, and its local data is deleted. To delete records held in SamoraOS, ask your organisation's SamoraOS admin or write to vasu@samoraglobal.com. The full Samora privacy policy is at samoraglobal.com/privacy.
